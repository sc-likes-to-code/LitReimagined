# Brand notes

**Mark:** an ember bookmark holding a quill whose tip sparks a small circuit line: classic craft, modern intelligence.

**Wordmark:** "Lit" in Fraunces with *Reimagined* in italic ember gradient.

**Assets**
- `public/brand/logo-mark.svg`: full-colour mark (for dark backgrounds)
- `public/brand/logo-mono.svg`: single-colour mark with the quill cut out
- `app/icon.svg`, `app/apple-icon.tsx`, `app/opengraph-image.tsx`: favicon, touch icon and social card

**Colour:** Ember `#FF5A36` to `#FF9A3C`, Circuit `#4CC9F0` (AI / live), Blush `#E9B8A6`, Dusty `#8FA9C4`, Sage `#9DB39A`. Ink `#0E0C14`, Paper `#F4ECDD`.

**Type:** Fraunces (display), Inter (interface), Newsreader (reading).

**Voice:** warm, literate, a little playful; never gimmicky. AI-generated text is always labelled.
